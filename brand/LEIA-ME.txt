GEOMETRIC WID — BRAND KIT
What I Did

VERSION
November 25, 2026
Vector reconstruction of the selected geometric WID concept.

1. FULL LOGO
Folder: 01-logo
SVG versions in graphite, black and white.
Transparent PNGs at widths of 2048 and 4096 pixels.
Letters and clock hands are vector shapes; no font installation is required.

2. ICON
Folder: 02-icone
The symbol is the D with a clock taken from the logo itself.
SVG versions in graphite, black and white.
Square transparent PNGs at 256, 512 and 1024 pixels.

3. WEB AND APP
Folder: 03-web-app
favicon.svg: white symbol on a graphite background with rounded corners.
favicon.ico: 16, 32, 48 and 64 pixel sizes in one file.
favicon-16/32/48/64.png: individual browser sizes.
The favicon clock hands have adjusted thickness and length for small sizes.
wid-app.svg and wid-app-192/512/1024.png: icon with an opaque graphite background and safe margin.
apple-touch-icon.png: opaque 180 × 180 pixel version.
App icons have a full square background; the platform may apply its own mask.

4. PREVIEW
Folder: 04-previa
Overview of the logo and symbol on light and dark backgrounds.

COLORS
Primary graphite: #1A1A1A / RGB 26, 26, 26.
Pure black: #000000 / RGB 0, 0, 0.
White: #FFFFFF / RGB 255, 255, 255.
Suggested light presentation background: #F7F7F3.

USAGE
Use SVG for interfaces, vector materials and applications that need to scale.
Use PNG when the destination tool requires a raster image.
Use graphite or black on light backgrounds and white on dark backgrounds.
Preserve the width-to-height ratio.
Leave clear space around the logo of at least 1/4 of the height of the letter I.
Allow for this clear space in the application, in addition to the file's existing margin.
Check the clock hands for legibility when the full logo is less than 400 pixels wide.
Prefer the icon and prepared favicons for very small applications.

TRANSPARENCY
PNGs in 01-logo and 02-icone have true transparency, including the space inside the D.
White artwork may appear empty on white; view it on a dark background.
App files and the preview intentionally include a background.

VECTOR STRUCTURE
Logo and icon SVGs contain vector paths, with no embedded bitmap,
external font, script or network dependency.
SVGs are master files that can be edited in vector editors.

Original asset filenames are preserved for compatibility with existing paths.
